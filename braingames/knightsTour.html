Knight's Tour game

The single coloured square represents the position of your Knight, make Knight's moves to travel around the board.
Knight's move: 2 squares away and the 1 to the side or 1 square orthogonal and 1 square diagonal.

Knight's Tour
You must travel to every square visiting each square once and only once...

Knight's Speed Tour
You must travel to every square on the board as quickly as you can - you can repeat squares in this version - just do it quickly!
