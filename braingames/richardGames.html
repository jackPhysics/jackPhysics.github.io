Richard II or Colour Pelmanism:

You click on any tile, and then you must click on a tile of the exact same colour - there are only 2 tiles of each colour on the board. But different colours can be very similar...
If you chose 2 the same they are cleared, otherwise you lose that turn. The aim is to clear the whole board with no excess clicks, scoring the perfect 100% - to keep your crown.
Make mistakes and your final score will be less than 100% - watch out, that knave Bolingbroke is trying to steal your crown - your final score is the chance you keep it...
