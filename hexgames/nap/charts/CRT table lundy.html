Attacks at less than 1-5 have no effect.

Attacks at greater than 6-1 are treated as 6-1.



KEY:
n - Attacker (A) or defender (D) must lose n factors. These can all be lost by a single unit (if it has at least n factors to lose), or by up to n units losing 1 factor each, or by any combination that adds up to n factors

By a player's own choice: instead of losing 1 factor, all their own units involved in the combat may retreat 1 hex each.

By a player's own choice: instead of losing 1 factor, ONE of their own units involved in the combat may be 'disrupted'.

'Disrupted' units have their effective CF and MF halved (rounding upwards).
To recover from disruption a unit must do nothing during a turn except recover - this counts as it's movement for the turn; and obviously it must occur outside of enemy ZOC, as if it is in enemy ZOC it must attack that turn, and so cannot un-disrupt.

There is no additional effects to CRT due to any of the defending units being disrupted, except a unit cannot be disrupted twice - it must retreat and/or lose factors.

?Artillery operate in a different way. They do not take part in normal combat. They can only do 'bombardment' attacks. For normal attacks they have an effective combat value of 0. If ever attacked by adjacent enemy units artillery units are automatically disrupted.

Artillery units cannot be destroyed, they can only be disrupted. Disrupted artillery cannot fire and cannot move. They can only undisrupt if there is an undisrupted friendly unit adjacent to them at the end of their turn and no adjacent enemy units.
