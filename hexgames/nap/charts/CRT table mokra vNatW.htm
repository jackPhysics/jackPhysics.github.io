Attacks at less than 1-4 have no effect.

Attacks at greater than 4-1 are treated as 4-1.



KEY:
n - defender must lose n steps. These can be totalled out of any of the defending units in the hex

Any defending units that lose steps while in clear terrain or on embankment hex are 'disrupted'

'Disrupted' units have no ZOC, not even over the hex they occupy - enemy units can move straight through them, but cannot stop on the hex they occupy.
They cannot attack.
They have full movement allowance, but cannot move into enemy ZOC.
At the end of their turn any undisrupted units not adjacent to enemy units (even disrupted enemy units), or not adjacent to enemy ZOC hexes can undisrupt - otherwise stay disrupted another turn, even if now no-longer in clear terrrain.
There is no additional effects to CRT due to any of the defending units being disrupted.
