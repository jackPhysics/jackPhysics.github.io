Choose your game:

For all the 'tile' games (sudoku, dominoes, code-breaker, nomograms) you can zoom out by pressing '1' and zoom in by pressing '2', in orde to see the whole board.

'Sudoku' games

Randomly generated boards
- different levels of difficulty...
from easy to impossible (the boards are randomly generated, and so may not give enough data, but there are hint buttons available)

'Dominoes' puzzle game

Move the dominoes to cover the whole board - there is a "HINTS!" button if you need some help (you will!)
Suggestion: give yourself a hint to start and then up to 2 more when you get stuck

'Code Breaker' puzzle game

Can you crack the code? Set your own level of difficulty... from 'easy' to 'insane'...

'Hanjie' or 'Nomogram' puzzle game

Unlike the usual game which generates a picture, these do not. They generate a random selection of black and white squares. But the challenge is the same - decode the space. You can choose the size but the difficulty is random - it might be easy, it might be impossible...

'Pelman' or 'Pelmanism' memory game

From a few cards (seconds) to very many cards (hours!)...

Rock Paper Scissors

Simple (very old! 2002!) R/P/S game using HTML (HTML-4) and javascript - you vs a random engine

'Gorillas' games

Based on the Qbasic classic.
Projectile motion - choose the angle and speed of your throw and try to hit the other gorilla before he hits you
- different version available...

Legion

'Legion' - a game of my own invention...
